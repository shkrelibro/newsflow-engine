Method

Headline and outlet only. The engine does not fetch article bodies. Where an original-language line appears above a translation, that is the headline as published, not a quotation from the article. Nothing here is inferred from text that was not read.

Links route through Google News where the outlet link was not resolved. Redirect resolution is off on GitHub Actions because it costs more than the run budget allows.

Name-bearing test. The engine issues grouped OR queries across several names at a time, so a cluster can attach to a name whose headline never mentions it. Those {rA_inherit} tier-A rows are held and searchable but not published, because publishing them is how a brief becomes noise.

This page is rendered by scripts/build_brief.py from the engine's own exports. Every count above is computed, not typed. Judgement is confined to the ranking of the lead and the commentary under each item.
